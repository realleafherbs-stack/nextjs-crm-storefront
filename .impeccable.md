## Design Context

### Users

Israeli, Hebrew-speaking shoppers comparing and purchasing professional hair clippers, trimmers, and shavers. Many visits are mobile and purchase-oriented. Users need to understand the product quickly, trust the official importer and warranty, and complete a purchase without interface elements obscuring the product or checkout path.

### Brand Personality

Premium, precise, restrained. The experience should feel confident and professional, with the clarity of a specialist grooming brand rather than the visual noise of a discount marketplace.

### Aesthetic Direction

An editorial, industrial-luxury system built from warm black, ivory, muted stone, and a controlled gold accent. Typography is bold where it establishes product hierarchy, while controls, notices, and utility layers remain compact and quiet. RTL layouts should feel intentional. Avoid oversized overlays, generic rounded cards, excessive decoration, and UI that competes with product imagery.

### Design Principles

1. Keep the product and primary purchase action visually dominant.
2. Design mobile-first for fast scanning, comfortable touch targets, and minimal obstruction.
3. Use gold as a precise accent, not as a large decorative field.
4. Prefer compact, progressive disclosure for privacy, accessibility, and other utility controls.
5. Preserve clear hierarchy, readable Hebrew, keyboard focus, and accessible contrast at every size.
